        How to play

        Everyone throws at once

        Stone blunts scissors. Scissors cut paper. Paper wraps stone. The same shape is a draw.

        Online with friends

        One person hosts and gets a room code and a QR code. Everyone else scans it or types the code on their own phone. Up to 6 play; anyone else who joins watches. Each round runs on a 5-second clock (the host can pick 3 or 10). The cards turn the moment the last person throws, and anyone too slow gets a random throw. Nobody sees a throw before then, and you can change yours until it turns.

        Two players

        Play one throw, or best of 3, 5 or 7. Draws are thrown again.

        Groups: points

        Score 1 for every player you beat. With three stones, two scissors and a paper, each stone scores 2 and the paper scores 3. Most points after the last round wins. A tie at the top goes to a throw-off.

        Groups: knockout

        A throw counts only when exactly two shapes show. Everyone on the losing shape is out. Last one standing wins. Handy for picking who goes first.

        One phone, many people

        Each person picks in secret, then passes the phone on. Cards stay face down until the last person has picked.

        Bots

        Bots lock in before anyone picks, using only earlier rounds. Easy has habits you can spot. Fair throws at random. Sharp studies each player’s habits and throws the counter.

        The unbeatable plan

        Throw each shape a third of the time, truly at random. Nobody can beat that in the long run, and it can’t beat anyone either. People aren’t random, which is how you win.
